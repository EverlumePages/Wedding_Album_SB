Chapter by Chapter

Our Story

From the nervous butterflies of the morning to the last dance under the stars — every moment, woven into one unforgettable day.

The Day Unfolded

June 15th dawned golden over the Tuscan hills. The air was sweet with wildflowers, and the whole world seemed to hold its breath in anticipation. Here is how our love story unfolded that day, moment by beautiful moment.

8:00 AM

Getting Ready

The morning began with soft laughter and champagne bubbles. Dushani's hands trembled with excitement as her veil was placed just so. In the next room, Kasun straightened his tie for the hundredth time, the biggest smile on his face.

9:30 AM

Every Little Detail

From the hand-written vows folded in his breast pocket to the "something blue" sewn into the hem of her dress — every detail was a love letter to each other. The rings gleamed softly, ready for their moment.

11:00 AM

The First Look

Time stood still. Kasun turned to see Dushani walking toward him, radiant in the morning light. His eyes glistened. Her smile broke into laughter. It was the moment that set the tone for everything that followed.

2:00 PM

Walking Together

Hand in hand, step by step, they walked into their forever. The aisle seemed to stretch toward infinity, lined with the faces of everyone who had witnessed their love grow. Dushani's veil danced in the gentle breeze.

2:30 PM

The Vows

"I promise to be your companion, your partner, and your best friend..." The words came out shaking, honest, perfect. Tears fell freely — from them, from everyone. This was the moment hearts became one.

2:45 PM

The Kiss

"You may now kiss..." — and the world erupted. The kiss was tender, the cheers were thunderous, and the confetti filled the sky like a thousand tiny prayers taking flight. Married. Finally, beautifully, forever married.

5:00 PM

Let's Celebrate

The reception was pure magic. Fairy lights twinkled overhead, speeches brought both tears and laughter, and the dance floor never emptied. Every hug, every clink of glasses — a celebration of love itself.

7:00 PM

Golden Hour

As the sun painted the sky in amber and rose, Dushani and Kasun stole away for a quiet moment together. These golden hour portraits are perhaps the most tender — two people, newly married, perfectly in love, perfectly at peace.

"And they lived happily ever after..."
